=========================================
ROAD OBSTRUCTION RECEIVER (ESP8266) PIN CONNECTIONS
=========================================

1. SX1278 (Ra-01 / Ra-02) LoRa Module
-----------------------------------------
Wemos Pin     LoRa Pin    Notes
-----------------------------------------
3V3           VCC         Use 3.3V, NOT 5V!
G             GND         Ground
D5 (GPIO14)   SCK         SPI Clock
D6 (GPIO12)   MISO        SPI MISO
D7 (GPIO13)   MOSI        SPI MOSI
D8 (GPIO15)   NSS (CS)    Chip Select
D0 (GPIO16)   RST         Reset
D3 (GPIO0)    DIO0        Interrupt (essential)

2. I2C LCD (16x2)
-----------------------------------------
Wemos Pin     Component   Notes
-----------------------------------------
D1 (GPIO5)    SCL         I2C Clock
D2 (GPIO4)    SDA         I2C Data
3V3 or 5V     VCC         Depends on your LCD module (most take 5V)
G             GND         Ground

3. Outputs (Buzzer)
-----------------------------------------
Wemos Pin     Component   Notes
-----------------------------------------
D4 (GPIO2)    Buzzer +    Active buzzer positive pin
G             Buzzer -    Buzzer ground
